Github io

This is the default site for all .github.io subdomains. Create a github.io site by enabling Pages in your repo settings.

HOW TO DO IT

Your repo just needs a good connection to GitHub Pages!

Go to your repository settings. Then select Pages. You must select a branch (like main or your custom). Then, it will generate the site. It will be hosted at https://yourusername.github.io/project-site.

As the owner of the repository, you keep complete control over your published content. But that also means that all rights and duties that come along with publishing a website remain in your responsibility.
